Frequently Asked Questions

CineMatch runs a hybrid recommendation engine. It combines Collaborative Filtering (identifying users with matching tastes to suggest what they loved) and Content-Based Filtering (scoring movies according to your preferred genres) to achieve highly personalized matching percentages.

Your reviews and CineMatch community ratings are on a 5-star scale (1 to 5). Critical scores pulled from international platforms (like IMDb) are represented on their native 10-point scale for comparative reference.

Syncing your identity secures your ratings, reviews, and watchlist history. It automatically links your data to the cloud database, allowing you to access your personal recommendations across any device seamlessly.
